# 3.2.4 Functional Requirements

This subsection presents the core features and functionalities of the proposed **Web-Based Bus Transportation Management System with Digital Remittance Recording and AI-Supported Passenger Demand Forecasting**. It defines how the system processes user inputs and supports operational workflows of the Audit Teller and Management.

---

## 1. Conductors

**1.1** The system shall allow conductors to securely log in using assigned credentials provided by the owner/secretary.

**1.2** The system shall enable conductors to view their assigned trips, schedules, and bus details for operational guidance.

**1.3** The system shall allow conductors to encode and print passenger tickets or receipts using the integrated ticketing and thermal printing module.

**1.4** The system shall allow conductors to record passenger counts by fare category (e.g., regular, student, senior citizen, PWD, discounted) during trips for accurate fare computation and record keeping.

**1.5** The system shall enable conductors to submit trip expenses and operational reports to the audit teller for verification.

---

## 2. Drivers

**2.1** The system shall allow drivers to securely log in using credentials assigned by the owner/secretary.

**2.2** The system shall enable drivers to view their assigned bus, route, and trip schedules for daily operational guidance.

**2.3** The system shall provide drivers with trip information such as departure time, destination, and assigned conductor.

---

## 3. Audit Teller

**3.1** The system shall provide a secure login for the audit teller using credentials assigned by the owner/secretary.

**3.2** The system shall allow the audit teller to verify, approve, and finalize remittance records submitted by conductors.

**3.3** The system shall compare expected net gross against actual cash collected and identify any discrepancies.

**3.4** The system shall compute deductions of expenses from gross income, calculate driver and conductor income, and determine passenger revenue by multiplying counts per category by their corresponding fare rates.

**3.5** The system shall maintain a complete audit trail of all transactions which are the submitted remittance report records.

---

## 4. Owner

**4.1** The system shall allow the owner to securely log in using assigned credentials provided.

**4.2** The system shall allow the owner to review and monitor approved remittance records finalized by the audit teller.

**4.3** The system shall allow the owner to add, update, and manage bus information such as bus number, plate number, and capacity.

**4.4** The system shall allow the owner to create and manage trip schedules, including routes, departure times, and assigned personnel.

**4.5** The system shall allow the owner to export reports in downloadable formats.

**4.6** The system shall allow the owner to access and view dashboards displaying operational and financial data.

**4.7** The system shall utilize validated historical data to generate passenger demand forecasts using predictive analytics.

**4.8** The system shall securely store all approved transactions and operational records in the database.
